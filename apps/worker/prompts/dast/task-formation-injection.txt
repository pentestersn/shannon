<class_boundary>
One task is one attacker-controlled input reaching one observed dangerous behavior in one injection context. Split independently controlled inputs, different contexts, or materially different defenses.

Read `source`, `combined_sources`, `path`, and `sink_call` as one observed data flow — `sink_call` carries observed behavior and labels any inferred sink as inferred. Use `slot_type` and `sanitization_observed` to distinguish the injection context and its observed defense. A shared sink, CWE, endpoint, payload, impact, or fix is supporting evidence only. Group only when one proof against one controlled input and observed behavior would settle every observation with one verdict.
</class_boundary>
